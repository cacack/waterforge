### Is this the real problem? (55-min / 5 Whys)
The panel's frame is "the honest posture hasn't reached the stranger-facing copy." That's true, and it's cosmetic. Drill the new load-bearing claim instead:

- Why is maintenance justified? Because the maintainer actively uses the app (ADR 0016, decision 1).
- How do we know? ADR 0016 says the claim "is verifiable."
- Has anyone verified it? No. The only instrument the project has is the beacon in `docs/operations/traffic-baseline.md`. Over 92 days it recorded about 40 page loads, on 4 distinct days, all desktop, and all "consistent with the maintainer and a single passer-by." There is no mobile load and no evidence of use "at the counter," which Principle 6 names as its test.

So the posture moved the justification from an unprovable audience to a "verifiable" maintainer, then never checked it. The data it does have doesn't corroborate it. There may be innocent reasons: the maintainer's browser may block the beacon, installed-PWA loads while offline won't report, and local dev runs don't count. But nobody has written any of that down.

The second root issue is that the backlog is now generated by reviews, not by use. Two product panels and one engineering panel ran in 15 days. 36 non-merge commits since 2026-09-11 are dependency bumps or trace to panels. None traces to "this annoyed me at the counter." The governance apparatus is the most active user of the project.

### Pre-mortem
It's 2027-09 and this failed. The most likely causes:
- **The maintainer stopped using it, and nothing noticed.** Every dashboard stayed green, because they all measure the site's health and none measures its use. Layer 1 lapsed silently, and the project kept running on layer 3 without anyone deciding to switch. *Prevented by:* a use signal (the log, or an explained beacon) plus a written trigger, e.g. "no personal use in 2 quarters → declare artifact mode and drop to Dependabot plus advisories only."
- **Governance fatigue.** Each panel produces about 10 small, virtuous issues. The maintainer tires of the meta-work, not the dependencies, and walks away. *Prevented by:* a bounded review cadence and a findings bar.
- **A wrong number reaches someone.** At about 40 loads a quarter the risk is low, but the template to report one exists and there is no best-effort caveat beside it. *Prevented by:* the trust fix (one line), which is worth doing on its own merits.
